The 2026 theme

Innovating is building bridges

Innovation does not happen in isolation. It grows when we create connections, share experiences and recognize the strength that already exists in the Pampa.

Bridge 1

Between those who produce and those who research

Bringing knowledge closer to the region's real needs by connecting universities, researchers, producers and companies.

Bridge 2

Between those who build businesses and those who invest

Taking people with an idea to those who can help make it real, in meetings that generate business.

Bridge 3

Between the countryside and the city

Recognizing the strength of agribusiness and bringing it closer to technology, research and new solutions.

Bridge 4

Between knowledge and opportunities

Making innovation accessible to students, business owners, producers, entrepreneurs and the whole community.

Bridge 5

Between the present and the future

Valuing the knowledge that already exists in the Pampa and connecting it to new technologies.

New bridge

Between AI and innovation

Artificial intelligence cuts across every sector. Understand how it changes the ecosystem and how to use it in our region.

Program highlights

01

Mostra Tecnológica (Technology Showcase)

18 short talks on careers, communication, entrepreneurship, games and AI. Both days, 9:00–18:00.

A full day to turn the region's challenges into solutions, with awards in the late afternoon. October 30, 8:30–18:00.

05

Diálogos (Dialogues) Alegrete Summit

Where are we? Where are we going? From the Summit to business. Three sessions with leaders, a close look at China and cases from Alegrete. October 29, from 19:00.

06

Talks and workshop

Contact Pró, Humanized Innovation with Marcus Rossi (Gramado Summit) and the workshop Video for Social Media. October 30, from 18:45.
