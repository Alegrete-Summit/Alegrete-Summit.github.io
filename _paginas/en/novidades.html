São Paulo Mission · September 2026

37 people, 14 municipalities, all four helices

Government, business, academia and civil society traveled together for an immersion in science, technology and entrepreneurship. Alegrete was represented by Jéssica Luz and Edson Fumagalli.

At CPQD, CNPEM/Sirius, IMATech, InovaUSP, Cubo and Inovabra, the delegation discussed:

  • Artificial intelligence applied to public administration and business, with organized processes and human oversight.
  • Using data and indicators to support decisions and improve municipal services.
  • Connecting scientific research and entrepreneurship, with support for startups and access to laboratories.
  • Open innovation and partnerships among universities, companies and government.
  • Communities that bring talent, challenges and opportunities together.

These topics inspire the special on AI and innovation ecosystems.
