Clip 4 · true story

The AI revolution has already begun. And it is running us over

In the IoTEdu innovation project, a team of 6 good undergraduate and graduate students spent 7 days, about 256 person-hours, on a task. A single undergraduate using LLMs at a high level delivered the same result, better and more complete, in 2 hours. AI does not level the field: it widens the gap. A message to computing professors and to companies.

Clip 1 script

Seven scenes, one idea

Green is artificial intelligence. Amber is innovation: people, ideas and territory. Where the two meet, the colors blend.

  1. Opening

    Circuit lines grow from the corners and form the Alegrete Summit triangle.

  2. Two organisms

    A green neural network and an amber organic shape. "AI without innovation loses its purpose. Innovation without AI loses its speed."

  3. The double helix

    The two intertwine: AI + Innovation, the indissoluble symbiosis. Neither moves forward without the other.

  4. The Pampa

    Coxilhas (rolling hills), a low sun, an umbu tree and the rice paddies. Eight sectors of the region light up and connect.

  5. The cycle

    Data, models, decisions, people, new problems. AI learns from data; innovation learns from people; human oversight goes along with every turn.

  6. The bridge

    Government, business, academia and society hold up an arch. Innovating is building bridges. AI is the new bridge.

  7. Closing

    Alegrete Summit 2026, October 29 and 30, Sicredi Essência, Alegrete, RS, Brazil.

Animation and soundtrack generated with code, no stock images. The narrated versions use a synthetic (neural) voice, in Portuguese, English and Spanish. The production script is in tools/clipe/ in the website repository.
