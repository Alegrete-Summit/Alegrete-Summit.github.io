The role of AI in innovation ecosystems

Artificial intelligence is no longer just one sector of the economy. It is a layer that cuts across all of them, as electricity and the internet did before it. In an innovation ecosystem, it changes how fast ideas become tests, tests become products and products reach people.

The symbiosis

Neither moves forward without the other

In biology, symbiosis is a relationship in which two organisms depend on each other to thrive. It is the best word to describe AI and innovation today.

What AI gives innovation

Speed and scale

  • Prototypes in days, not months: texts, screens, code and analyses move past the draft stage quickly.
  • Skills that once required a team come within reach of a small business.
  • Data turns into patterns, forecasts and alerts instead of forgotten spreadsheets.
  • Technical knowledge becomes accessible in plain language, in the asker's own language.
+
What innovation gives AI

Purpose and grounding

  • Real problems, chosen by those who live in the region. Without them, AI is a tool looking for a use.
  • Local data: a model trained in another biome does not understand a rice várzea (lowland floodplain) in the Pampa.
  • Business model, adoption and trust, which no algorithm creates on its own.
  • Governance and human oversight: someone decides, signs off and is accountable for the result.

In one sentence: AI without innovation loses its purpose; innovation without AI loses its speed. An ecosystem that treats them separately wastes both.

The four helices

What changes in each helix

Innovation ecosystems rest on four helices: government, business, academia and civil society. AI changes the role of each one and, above all, the bridges between them.

PP

Government

Faster services for citizens, triage of requests, indicators to decide where to invest. It requires organized data, transparency, compliance with the LGPD (Brazil's data protection law) and human oversight in every decision that affects people.

EM

Business

Productivity in customer service, marketing and management; new data-driven products. For a small business, AI works like an expanded team, as long as someone checks what it delivers.

AC

Academia

Applied research with computer vision and local models, talent development and laboratories open to startups. The university stops being only a source of knowledge and becomes a product partner.

SC

Civil society

Communities that teach and learn together, digital inclusion, girls in technology. This is the helix that ensures AI serves the people of the region, and not the other way around.

Five functions

What AI does inside an ecosystem

1

Shortens the idea → test cycle

An Ideathon that once ended on poster board can now end with a clickable prototype. Cheap testing changes who gets to innovate.

2

Democratizes skills

Writing, data analysis, translation into the Spanish of the border, basic programming: skills that used to be concentrated come within reach of anyone with a good question.

3

Connects knowledge

University research can be summarized and explained to those who produce; producers' experience can become data for those who research. AI is a translator between helices.

4

Turns data into decisions

Satellite images, field sensors, municipal indicators. MapBiomas, for example, uses machine learning to map the Pampa year by year since 1985.

5

Creates new businesses

Startups applying AI to agriculture, public administration, health and education. PampaTec, its incubator and the acceleration programs are the way in.

!

And it demands care

Errors that look like right answers, bias from outside data, privacy, vendor dependence and energy consumption. See the precautions below.

The precautions

AI with human oversight

The São Paulo Mission, which took 37 people from 14 municipalities in Rio Grande do Sul to centers such as CPQD, CNPEM/Sirius, InovaUSP and Cubo, came back with one central idea: AI applied to public administration and business, with organized processes and human oversight.

Errors that look like right answers

A model for diagnosing plant diseases got 99% of the photos from the dataset it was trained on right, and about 31% of photos taken under other conditions, without losing confidence in its answers (Mohanty, Hughes and Salathé, Frontiers in Plant Science, 2016). The same applies to the Pampa: a model from elsewhere needs local data.

A person is accountable

AI assistants can make up sources, numbers and quotes that look real. Every output that becomes a decision, document or product needs someone to check it, sign off on it and be accountable for it.

Data belongs to the region

Data about citizens, customers and rural properties has owners and is protected by the LGPD. A mature ecosystem combines open data where possible with protection where necessary.

What we already have

The Pampa's foundation for AI

  • Universities and institutes: Unipampa, IFFar and Urcamp train people in computing, engineering, agronomy and management.
  • PampaTec: a technology park, a startup incubator and the Startup Pampa Acceleration Program.
  • Local applied research: Unipampa, together with Embrapa Pecuária Sul, already uses computer vision to identify capim-annoni (an invasive African lovegrass) and cattle ticks.
  • Support for entrepreneurs: Sebrae, Local Innovation Agents, Inova RS Fronteira Oeste e Campanha, Sicredi.
  • Engaged government: the City Hall and SEDETUR lead the Alegrete Cidade Inovadora (Alegrete Innovative City) ecosystem.
  • Communities: Technovation Gurias, NoDUS, content creators and the Summit's volunteer network.
