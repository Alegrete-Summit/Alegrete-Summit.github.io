Kauã's fable

Internet, a laptop and an AI model: the power to create reaches the heart of the Amazon.

This is a fable. Kauã and the Kaá Sentinel are a fictional character and product, created to tell an idea. The idea itself is very real: initiatives around the world already use reused phones to listen to forests.

A

A guardian with no signal

Kauã is a young Indigenous man who lives in a village in the heart of the Amazon. He knows every trail, every plant and every sound of the forest: the howler monkey's roar, the coming rain, the song of the uirapuru.

But the forest is in danger. At night, the roar of illegal chainsaws comes from far away, and sometimes the sky turns red with fires. By the time someone gets there, the tree has already fallen. And the village is cut off from the world: there is no way to call for help in time.

B

The internet comes down from the sky

One day, a satellite internet dish, like Starlink's, arrives in the village. Up above, hundreds of low-orbit satellites pass by all the time, and one of them always sees the village.

No cables, no cell towers: the signal goes through the canopy and reaches the middle of the forest. For the first time, the village is connected to the world.

C

A laptop in the right hands

A digital inclusion project gives Kauã a laptop. He has never studied computing. He has never written a line of code.

But he has two things no course teaches: curiosity and a real problem, which he knows better than anyone.

Claude · AI model
  1. In my village we hear the chainsaw from far away, but by the time we get there the tree has fallen. Can a phone listen to the forest for us?
  2. Yes, it can! Let's plan it together: old phones as ears, a solar panel for power, a model that recognizes sounds and an alert that reaches the village.
  3. I know the sounds of the forest: howler monkeys, rain, toucans, thunder… and the noise of chainsaws and fire.
  4. That knowledge is the most important part. Record examples of each sound and I'll help you write the code that trains the model.
  5. if sound == "chainsaw" or sound == "fire":
      alert_village(place, time)
  6. Test 1: false alarm with thunder ✗ · more thunder examples · Test 2: 97% accuracy ✓
Kauã: knowledge of the forestAI: the technique
D

A conversation that becomes code

Kauã opens an AI model, like Claude, and describes the problem his own way, in his own words. The AI asks questions, proposes a plan and helps him code, test and fix what goes wrong.

It is a partnership: Kauã brings the knowledge of the forest; the AI brings the technique. Neither could do it alone.

E

The Kaá Sentinel is born

Kaá means forest in Tupi. The Kaá Sentinel is made of reused phones with a small solar panel, fixed high up in the trees. They listen to the forest day and night.

An AI model recognizes the sound of chainsaws and fire and alerts the village within seconds. The guardians arrive before the tree falls. Illegal deforestation in the region plummets.

E

From the village to the world

Word spreads. Other communities, NGOs, governments and companies all over the world want the Kaá Sentinel. Forests in Africa, Asia and Central America start being listened to by Kauã's phones.

In a few months, the product becomes an international hit, and Kauã, who had never coded before, becomes a millionaire.

F

The money goes back to the village

With what he earned, Kauã invests where it all began.

  • Solar power in the homes
  • Clean water for everyone
  • A health post
  • A connected school
  • Other young people from the village building solutions

Now Kauã is not the only one. A young woman builds an app to monitor fishing in the river; a young man, a system to schedule the health boat. The village has become an innovation ecosystem.

The moral of the story

Internet and AI democratize the power to create.

Anyone, anywhere

With internet, a laptop and a sophisticated AI model, it is possible to create companies and products and to transform realities.

No longer a privilege

Building digital solutions is no longer exclusive to computer scientists or to people with a lot of money.

The local problem is the edge

Those who know the territory see the problem nobody else sees. Customizing and creating solutions for local problems can be one of the greatest advantages.

Knowledge + technique

AI brings the technique; people bring the knowledge, the context and the purpose. Together they go much further.
