Kauã and the Kaá Sentinel

Connect the village, build a solution by talking to the AI, protect the forest and transform the village with what the product earns. Based on Kauã's fable; fictional character and product.

Phase 1

Connect

Satellite internet reaches the village. Aim the dish at the low-orbit satellites.

Phase 2

Create with AI

Kauã brings the knowledge of the forest; the AI brings the technique.

Phase 3

Protect

Kaá sensors listen to the forest and send alerts within seconds. Answer them.

Phase 4

Invest

Success goes back to the village: power, water, health, school and new creators.
